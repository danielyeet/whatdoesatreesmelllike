# The Houses view as a helix round a central axis

Date: 2026-09-24

Files touched: `contact-sheet.js` (rewritten, ~735 lines), the `sheet-*` rules in
`style.css` (the hang's replaced by the axis's; the motifs' canvas raised from z-index 4
to 5), `tests/contact-sheet.spec.js` (the hang's tests replaced).

## What changed

The Houses view of Scent descriptions is no longer a gallery wall. The nine houses stand
on a **helix** of particles winding round a **central axis** down the middle of the page:
one house at the **front**, standing on the axis, large and sharp, with its line about the
house under it; the ones before and after it turned away round the axis above and below,
smaller and fainter the further round they are. You **travel** along the helix — by the
wheel, by dragging, by the arrow keys, by the house numbers standing on the axis, and by
the two buttons at the side with where you are between them. The page arrives with the
axis drawn out from the middle and the houses coming out of it one after another.

## Why it is built this way

### The helix

- **A house `d` houses from the front** stands `SPAN` of the page's height below it and
  `TURN` radians (62°) further round the axis (`spot()`): so the next house is turned away
  to the right and down, the one after it nearly behind the axis, and the ones before it go
  round the other way above. How near the eye it is (the cosine of its turn) decides how
  big it is drawn (down to `SMALLEST` right behind the axis), how faint, and whether it
  stands in front of the particles or behind them.
- **The front house stands on the axis**, and only it carries its line about the house
  (the **say**), which used to appear only while pointed at. A ring of specks turns round
  it (**the halo**).
- **Depth is z-index, three layers**: houses turned away behind the axis at 1, the
  particles' canvas at 2, the houses in front at 3 and the front house at 4. So the axis
  and the strands pass behind the house you are on and in front of the ones turned away.
  The houses behind are also softened and greyed a little (`.behind`). The motifs' canvas
  went from 4 to 5 to stay above all of them, and a house being rested on (`.hot`) is 6 —
  with `!important`, because the script writes every house's z-index inline.
- **Every house is the same size at the front** (`FRONT`, `SHAPE`), a picture cropped to
  fill it. The salon hang's many sizes went with it; on a helix the sizes come from depth.
- **On a phone the houses stand further apart** (`SPAN_NARROW`): the helix has no width
  there to turn the next house away in, and it stood on the front one. Only the edges of
  the houses before and after are seen now, top and bottom. Keyed to the width, as every
  phone fix on this site is.

### The particles

All on one canvas (`.sheet-field`), in the page's own ink on the white squared ground:

- **The axis** — a hairline down the middle, specks always **falling** down it, and ticks
  every quarter of a house that **travel as you do**, a long one at each house, so moving
  along the helix reads as moving along a ruler.
- **The strands** — two, either side of the axis, winding through where the houses stand,
  drawn nearer and heavier where they come towards the eye; bright specks **ride** them.
- **The tethers** — a line of specks from the axis out to every house, with a pulse
  running outward along it.
- **The dust** — a column of specks turning slowly round the axis at their own heights and
  reaches (`DUST`, fewer on a phone), **turning with the helix as you travel**, which is
  what makes travelling read as the whole thing turning rather than the pictures sliding.
- **The halo** round the front house, and **bursts** of specks thrown out as each house
  arrives.
- Specks near the pointer are drawn plainer.

The canvas runs only while the Houses view is on the page (the loop stops when the other
view is showing and is woken when it comes back), and draws at the lower ratio below
700px. With reduced motion nothing falls, turns or rides: it is drawn still and redrawn
when you travel.

### Travelling — the owner's "navigatable"

- **The wheel** moves along the helix, not down the page (`WHEEL`), and it comes to rest
  on the nearest house once the wheel stops (`SNAP_MS`). The page itself does not scroll:
  the houses have the window below the chrome, and no more.
- **A drag** — a finger on a phone, the mouse on a desktop — carries the helix with the
  hand, and comes to rest on the nearest house when let go. A press that was the end of a
  drag opens nothing. `touch-action: none` on the helix so a finger on it drags rather than
  scrolling the page.
- **The keys**: up and down (and left and right, and Page Up and Down) step one house;
  Home and End go to the ends. Tabbing to a house brings it to the front.
- **The numbers on the axis** — one per house, standing on the axis where that house is,
  travelling with it — are buttons that bring that house to the front.
- **The way round** (`.sheet-nav`) at the side: a button up, where you are (*04 / 09* and
  the house's name), a button down. Across the foot on a phone, on the chrome's ground.
- **Pressing a house at the side** brings it round to the front; **pressing the front
  one** steps the page back and opens it, as before.
- **The search** brings the first house it finds round to the front.

### The arrival

The axis is drawn out from the middle of the page (`AXIS_MS`), and the houses come out of
it one after another, **nearest the front first** (`EMERGE_STEP`), each travelling out
along its tether from the axis and fading up on a burst of specks. The front house only
fades up and grows very slightly where it stands: the test that the page never flashes its
own contents also holds that the first house is first drawn where it settles, and it must
not be seen to slide in. The chrome arrives once every house is out, as it did.

### Kept as it was

Resting on a house brings its motifs after a fifth of a second and blurs the rest
(`HOVER_WAIT_MS`), leaving lets them fade, pressing steps the page back before opening,
the number never written into the caption (the search reads the caption), the `js-coming`
hold, the page's own search, and the plain grid of links when the script is blocked.

## How to test it

```bash
npm test -- tests/contact-sheet.spec.js
```

Twenty-two tests. The axis's own, each proved against the fault it guards:

- **`the houses stand on a helix round a central axis, one at the front`** — one house at
  the front, the first, standing on the axis and the largest; the next off the axis, below
  and behind it; the nine numbers on the axis in order; and a column of ink down the middle
  of the canvas. Fails with the front house moved off the axis.
- **`the wheel, the keys, the numbers, the buttons and a drag all travel along the helix`**
  — and the page itself never scrolls. Fails with the wheel ignored.
- **`pressing a house at the side brings it to the front`** — fails when a press at the
  side opens the house.
- **`the houses come out of the axis one after another, nearest first`** — fails with
  every house arriving at once.
- **`the particles move on their own`** — fails with the particles' clock stopped.
- **`the front house and the way round stay inside the window at every width`** — at four
  widths down to a phone, with no sideways scroll and the way round never printed over the
  front house. Fails with the way round moved over it.
- **`the old startup flick is gone`**, and so are the hang's rail and wires.
- Carried over, changed only in how they reach a house (brought to the front with the
  keys first): resting brings the motifs after a moment — its "everything else goes out of
  focus" now reads the house beside the front one, since a house behind the axis is soft
  anyway — passing over the houses sets nothing off (swept back and forth across the
  houses that can be seen, since most are turned away), the motifs fade rather than
  vanish, every house's motifs draw, Qimu's staves, and pressing the front house steps the
  page back.

By hand: `npm run serve`, open `http://localhost:8123/categories/scent-descriptions.html`,
wheel, drag, press the numbers on the axis, rest on the front house.

## Known issues / TODO

- **On a phone the houses either side are cut by the window's edges**: the helix's reach
  is taken off the width, and on a phone that puts the neighbours half off the page. They
  are there to say there is more, not to be read.
- **The helix stops at the ends** rather than wrapping round from 09 to 01; the wheel and
  a drag give a little past either end and come back.
- **It does not remember where you were**: coming back to the page starts at 01.
