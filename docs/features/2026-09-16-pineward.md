its wood grown through the whole page in `8137578` and its parts made to open gradually
in `a02339d`, both 2026-09-17. Migrated from CLAUDE.md on 2026-09-17.

Files: `works/pineward.html`, `pineward.js` (~690 lines), the `pine-*` block in
`style.css`, `tests/pineward.spec.js`

## What it is

The first house in Scent descriptions, "the house that smells like trees", and the piece
with the most in it. A long page — an introduction and **fifty-four parts**, one per
fragrance, each a picture and the owner's own notes — and the layout is the answer to
that length.

The parts are in **alphabetical order by the fragrance's name**, and the writing in them
is the owner's: their spelling and punctuation are theirs, so don't tidy them. The
scores they originally carried ("9.5/10" at the end of an entry) were taken out at their
request; two are left where they are part of a sentence rather than a verdict on its
own.

## A part is compacted

Closed, a part is its number, a small picture and its title on a ruled line with a run
of dots between the title and the cue; open, it is the picture at size with the writing
beside it. Fifty-four of anything listed straight down a page is a wall.

It is a real `<details class="pine-part">`, so it opens and closes, takes the keyboard
and works without the script.

## Opening and closing a part

**Opening and closing one is a movement, not a cut** (`OPEN_MS` 760ms, `SHUT_MS` 620ms,
`PART_EASE`). A `<details>` does both in one frame on its own, which is right when
nothing is watching and wrong here — the owner asked for both halves to be "smooth and
gradual". So the summary's own click is caught and the element is opened or closed
around an animation:

- **Opening**, the element is opened at once (its contents have to be on the page to be
  measured), the box travels from nothing to the height it wants, and the writing comes
  up a beat later so the room is already opening before anything appears in it.
- **Closing**, the writing goes first and the box follows it down, and only when the box
  has closed is the element really shut. Shut it first and the browser takes the
  contents off the page in that frame, which is the cut this exists to avoid.

**The padding travels with the height.** Left behind, the last frame of closing is a
forty-pixel box with nothing in it that then disappears — a step at the end of a smooth
movement. It is read off the stylesheet rather than written in the script, so the two
cannot disagree.

[ADAR](2026-09-17-adar.md)'s fragrances open exactly the same way, from the same code
written twice: these pages share nothing by design.

## The four strata

The parts are grouped into four **strata** — Canopy, Understorey, Trunk, Roots — a
section through a forest read from the light down into the ground. The first two carry
**fourteen** each and the last two **thirteen**, which is what fifty-four divides into
four ways; it was four of thirteen when the page was written and the owner's list came
to fifty-four. The strata and the numbers are in the page's own markup, not worked out
in the script.

## The wood

**The wood is grown, and it runs the length of the piece.** Conifers stand down both
margins of the whole page rather than as one canopy behind the title: each is a straight
leader carrying whorls of branches (`WHORLS`) that shorten as they rise and droop as
they reach out (`BRANCH_DROOP`), with specks strung along them (`TWIG_EVERY`) and only
their near neighbours joined (`treeAt`, `branchOf`, `growWood`, `paintWood`). The owner
asked for the trees "extended and go through the entire page" and made "more
coniferous".

The whole page's trees are grown ONCE, in the page's own coordinates, and bucketed into
rows so that only the rows on screen are drawn — growing them per screenful instead
would re-roll the wood every time you scrolled. It grows from nothing over `GROW_MS`
(2.1s) when the page opens and then holds its shape, and it is kept out of the middle of
the page (`CLEAR_MID`, 0.42 of the width), where the writing stands.

**The web between the specks is short and capped** (`WEB_REACH` 26px, `WEB_EACH` 2).
Joining every speck within reach — the first version — came out as long lines striking
across the page and closing into triangles: a net thrown over the title rather than
something growing behind it. [The essay pages](2026-09-17-the-essay-pages.md) inherited
the small numbers from this mistake.

## It idles, and it blooms

`IDLE`, `IDLE_RATE`, `BLOOM_*`. A speck drifts about a pixel around its own place on its
own slow clock, so the wood is never quite still while never going anywhere — the owner
asked for exactly that: "the particles themselves are free to slightly idle, yet the
taught character should remain the same".

Near the pointer (within `BLOOM_REACH`, 150px) a speck is drawn more plainly and puts
out a few short needles, eased in and out (`BLOOM_EASE`) so the bloom follows the hand
rather than switching on and off with it. **Nothing about the drawing moves with the
pointer** — it is the same tree in the same place, drawn fuller where the hand is —
which is the other half of what they asked for: "this should not be interactive and
should not move with the mouse".

## The trunk

**The trunk is the piece's own scale**: one tick per part down the side, inked in as it
is passed, with the reading in the corner counting them. It reads the PARTS and not the
scrollbar — a part that runs long should not read as more of the piece than a part that
runs short — and it is taken off the page below 860px, where the window is the whole of
the room.

The reading names what fills the window rather than what was passed last (`NAMES_IT`,
0.45), the same way ADAR's does; the two are worth keeping in step.

## Without the script

The page is all of its writing. The trunk and the reading are added by the script and
the wood is drawn by it; none of them carry anything to read.

## How to test it

```bash
npm test -- tests/pineward.spec.js
```

Eight tests: the piece being an introduction and fifty-four parts in four strata of
fourteen, fourteen, thirteen and thirteen, numbered straight through in the markup; a
part being a title and a small picture until it is opened and its picture and writing
once it is; the trunk carrying one tick per part with the reading counting what has been
passed and letting go again on the way back up; the wood running the length of the piece
and being grown when the page opens; it idling where it stands and blooming under the
hand; the piece being simply there with animation turned off; all of its writing being
there without its script. `tests/contact-sheet.spec.js` checks the sheet's first picture
points at it.
