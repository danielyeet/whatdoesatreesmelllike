# The Houses view as a gallery hang

Date: 2026-09-24

Files touched: `contact-sheet.js` (rewritten again), `motifs.js` (Tombstone's and Qimu &
Musicians' sets replaced), the `sheet-*` rules in `style.css` (the chain's replaced by the
hang's), `tests/contact-sheet.spec.js` (the chain's tests replaced).

## What changed

The Houses view of Scent descriptions is a **gallery wall** now. A picture rail runs
across the wall, and each house hangs from it on a hook, **high and low in turn**, a
salon hang. Every picture is framed on a white mount and carries a **museum label**
under it: its number, large, and its name. The wall is **hung picture by picture** as the
page arrives, each picture lowered on to its hook and swinging to rest, and a picture
**swings** when the pointer brushes past it. Resting on a house still brings its
**motifs**, now after a fifth of a second rather than nearly half of one. Tombstone's
motifs are new, and Qimu & Musicians' are **music staves** now.

## Why it is built this way

### The hang

- **One rail, nine hooks, two tiers.** On a wide window all nine houses hang from one
  rail, their hooks evenly spaced along it, in their order from left to right. The odd
  places (01, 03, 05, 07, 09) hang **high**, close under the rail on two wires in a
  triangle, the way a picture hangs from a single hook. The even places hang **low**, on a
  long cord that drops **between** the two high pictures either side of it and opens
  into two wires just above the frame.
- **Nothing can collide, by construction.** A high picture may be no wider than two hook
  spacings less a margin, so it can never reach the cords either side of it. A low
  picture hangs below the lower of its two high neighbours' labels. And no picture is
  wider than the room between its hook and the nearer edge of the wall, which is what
  keeps the ones at the ends from hanging off the page (at 1024px they did, by 2px).
- **Fewer to a rail on a narrower window**: five on a mid-sized one, four, and three on a
  phone, shared out as evenly as they go (`perRail`). Each rail is its own hang.
- **Every picture is its own size and shape**, as in a salon hang: its width is a share
  of the most it may have, and its height a multiple of its width (`WIDE`, `TALL`).
  Seeded, so the wall is hung the same way every visit.
- **No picture is narrower than the longest word of its name**, measured in the label's
  own face. A long name at the end of a rail goes on to a second line, between its words.
  On a phone, names first broke in the middle of words ("Pinewar / d").
- **It waits for the page's faces before it hangs**, for at most 1.2 seconds. A name's
  width decides how wide its picture may be, and the first version laid the wall out
  again when the fonts arrived, which moved pictures already on their way down to their
  hooks. A test caught it.

### The label

A museum labels its wall, so every picture carries a label under it. The **number** comes
first and large (the owner asked for the numbering to be kept), with the **name** beside
it under a hairline. The line about the house (the say) still appears below when the
picture is pointed at.

**The number is not written into the caption.** It stood inside it for a moment, and the
page's own search, which reads the caption, then looked for "02ADAR". It stands beside
the caption now, as its own element.

### Hanging and swinging

- **The wall is hung as the page arrives**: the rail is drawn across first, then each
  picture is lowered on to its hook in order, 150ms apart. Each drops 70px on its wires
  over three quarters of a second, fading in as it comes, and arrives swinging. The
  chrome comes once every picture is up.
- **A picture is a pendulum on its hook** (`STIFF`, `DAMP`, `MOST`). A longer wire swings
  more slowly, as a real one does. The pointer passing across a picture pushes it the way
  the pointer is going, harder the lower down it is caught, and never beyond 4°. **Nothing
  moves that nothing has touched**: the loop runs only while something is swinging, and a
  picture at rest is exactly still. The owner has asked before for movement that read as
  jitter to be taken out.
- **The wires are drawn to where the frame's corners actually are**, every frame it
  moves, so a swinging picture's wires swing with it and a picture being lowered
  stretches its wires down from the hook.
- **The frame's transform is the script's alone.** It is written every frame something
  moves, so nothing in the stylesheet may transition it. The picture coming forward when
  it is pressed is done on the image inside it instead.

### Resting, pressing, and the way in

Unchanged from [the chain](2026-09-23-the-chain-and-its-motifs.md) apart from the wait:
**`HOVER_WAIT_MS` is 200ms**, down from 420 ("maybe make the delay before they come up
smaller; it felt too long"). That is still long enough that sweeping across the wall sets
nothing off, and there is a test that does exactly that sweep. A picture being rested on
stops being pushed, and comes a little off the wall with a deeper shadow.

### Tombstone's motifs, again

It was stones standing up out of mist; the owner asked for "something different". Now:

- **Epitaphs**: the house's own five names, *3 Feet 5*, *Evergrow*, *No Need to Come
  By*, *Sing at My Funeral*, *Sweet Coffin*, cut into the wall one letter at a time in an
  italic serif, with a pale edge under each letter as a cut in stone has, sometimes
  underlined, and worn away again.
- **Roots** creeping in from the edges of the page, mostly the bottom, branching as they
  go. **Red flowers** open at their tips, after the house's card for Evergrow: "Fade and
  flourish, forever growing".
- **Soil** drifting up, fine and brown.

### Qimu & Musicians' motifs, again

"5 lines like music sheets, and add ephemeral notes to that." Each **stave** is five lines
9px apart, drawn across the page from left to right, with bar lines where it reaches
them and a 4/4 at its head. No two staves stand on top of each other. **Notes** come and go on
them: crotchets, quavers, minims, and beamed pairs. Each sits on a line or in a space,
with ledger lines when it stands above or below, stays a second or three while drifting
a little to the left, and is gone. The records and the line of sound that were there
before are gone.

## How to test it

```bash
npm test -- tests/contact-sheet.spec.js
```

Twenty-one tests. The hang's own, each proved against the fault it guards:

- **`the houses hang from the rail in their order, high and low in turn`**: nine pictures
  left to right in number order, tiers alternating, every hook on the rail above the
  middle of its picture, and every picture's wires running from its hook to its own top
  edge. Fails with every picture hung high.
- **`no picture or label overlaps another, and no wire crosses another picture`**, at four
  widths down to a phone, with the page never scrolling sideways. Fails with the low
  pictures hung up at the rail. It also found the end pictures reaching 2px off the page.
- **`every label carries the house's number and name, and the pictures are of many
  sizes`**: 01 to 09, each number under its picture and before its name, and no name
  cut short.
- **`the wall is hung picture by picture, in order`**: fails with every picture hung at
  once.
- **`a picture brushed by the pointer swings on its hook, and settles`**: fails with the
  push taken out.
- **`the old startup flick is gone`**: no flick, and every picture only ever seen coming
  down on to its own hook. It is what caught the wall moving when the fonts arrived.
- **`resting on a house brings its motifs after a moment, and blurs the rest`**: the wait
  is timed in the page, between the pointer arriving and the wall going out of focus,
  and must be between 120 and 320ms. Fails with the old 420.
- **`Qimu & Musicians' motifs are five-line staves with notes on them`**: reads the
  motifs' canvas for five long, level, evenly spaced lines, and for ink besides them.
  Fails with the old motifs.
- And carried over: passing over the houses sets nothing off, the motifs fade rather
  than vanish, every house has motifs that draw, pressing steps the page back, and the
  search, heading, number, reduced-motion, no-flash and no-script tests. The search test
  caught the number inside the caption.

By hand: `npm run serve`, open `http://localhost:8123/categories/scent-descriptions.html`,
watch the wall being hung, brush the pointer across a picture, rest on each house.
