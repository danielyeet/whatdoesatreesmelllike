# The paper: wash, grid and grain

Date: 2026-09-11 (the repository's first commit), last worked on in `6530c5e` (*Send a
second shockwave outwards as the map falls in*) the same day. Migrated from CLAUDE.md on
2026-09-17.

Files: `paper.js` (~570 lines), `index.html`, `style.css`,
`tests/background-and-cursor.spec.js`

## What it is

`paper.js` draws the three decorative layers behind the landing page — the black **wash**,
the squared **grid** that bends around the 3D diagram, and the **static** (grain) — on
canvases at throttled rates (`GRID_MS`, `NOISE_MS`) rather than every frame. Going from
slide 2 to slide 3 the paper *gathers* rather than appears, revealed by the **curtain**: a
wipe from the top of the page downwards whose left and right edges run ahead of its middle,
so the sides fill in first and the middle of the page last. It also drives the grid's part
of the collapse — a shockwave, an outward ring and an even suction.

## Why / key decisions

Nothing about the paper's arrival may be a step. Three things had to be true for that, and
each is easy to undo:

- **The mask must be completely solid before it is taken off.** `setCurtain()` drops the
  mask altogether once it has served its purpose; if any part of the page is still
  feathering at that moment, that part fills in one frame. `CURTAIN_REACH` is sized so the
  sweep has run past the bottom of the page well before then. (Dropping the mask while it
  was still feathering was a real bug, and there is a regression test for it.)
- **`paperIn` is eased flat at both ends** (`smoother`), so there is no moment you can point
  at where the paper starts or finishes arriving. An exponent below 1 was quicker off the
  mark but began with a step.
- **The finest grid tier fades in rather than switching on.** It is skipped while the grid
  is still molten — four times the line count for something the warp hides anyway — but as
  a *threshold* that put a whole tier on screen in one frame.
- **The grain is never cut by the wipe.** The mask is set on `.paper-wash` and `.paper-grid`
  themselves, not on `.paper` around them, so `.paper-noise` is left out of it. Texture
  arriving along a moving edge is about the most noticeable thing a page can do, and no
  amount of feathering hides it. The grain comes up evenly over the whole window on its own
  later, gentler ramp (`NOISE_START` / `NOISE_SPAN`) and is the last of the paper to settle.

The curtain itself is three mask layers **added**, not intersected: one sweep down the page,
and a lobe growing out of each top corner (`CURTAIN_*`, `setCurtain()`).

During the collapse the grid gets three separate motions and they are not interchangeable:
**the shockwave** is a narrow ring closing on the centre on its own faster clock (`WAVE_*`);
**the outward ring** (`OUTWARD_*`) runs the other way at the same time, shoving the grid
outward while everything else pulls in — it is on, at `OUTWARD_STRENGTH` 58, and setting
that to 0 is how you would remove it; and **the suction** is the even, proportional inward
pull applied to the whole grid on top of the per-node dimples, which is what makes the grid
implode rather than merely dimple near the middle.

**The grid is redrawn at the full rate while the page moves** (2026-09-26, the owner's
"make the home page smoother when going from 2 to 3 and vice versa"). At rest it is redrawn
every `GRID_MS`; it was already drawn every frame while molten on arrival and while imploding
on the way out, but for the last stretch of the way in — molten gone, the page still gliding —
it dropped to the resting rate and stepped behind the map as the map glided into place. It is
now drawn every frame whenever the page has moved since the last one (`moving`, off
`lastTop`). The rest of that round is in [the slides' report](2026-09-11-the-landing-slides-and-exit.md).

The paper's arrival going 2 → 3 **used to be shaped as a duck's wake** — a V trailing back
from the middle of the page, `WAKE_HALF_ANGLE`. That was replaced by the top-down wipe
above, and neither the V nor `WAKE_HALF_ANGLE` is in the file any more. "The wake" now
means only the specks along a branch of [the node map](2026-09-11-the-node-map.md).

## How to test it

```bash
npm test -- tests/background-and-cursor.spec.js
```

That file covers the paper's arrival — top-down, sides ahead of the middle, completely
covering the page before its mask comes off, and the grain never cut by that mask — plus
the custom cursor. Two of its checks are regressions for specific fixed bugs (the mask
being dropped while still feathering; grain arriving along a moving edge), so keep them
passing rather than adjusting them to match new behaviour.

By hand: serve the site and scroll slide 2 → 3 slowly, watching the top corners and the
middle of the page for anything that arrives in one frame.
